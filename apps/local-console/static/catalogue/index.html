Risk & safety

The most important distinction in the product: an inferred watch is not a confirmed hazard, and the interface never lets the two look alike. A confirmed hazard comes only from a certified alarm.

Risk banners
Informational — Utility humidity has been rising for three days.
Watch — advisory — Unusual electrical draw in the utility room. SYLTRA has not confirmed a fault and will not act on this alone.
Confirmed — gas alarm, kitchen — Certified detector reporting. Follow your household safety procedure.

Critical-action confirmation

For locks, valves, breakers, sirens, security modes and emergency responses. §21 lists ten things this must show and eight patterns it must not use. The SYLTRA console commands no actuators today, so this is not wired to anything — it exists here so the first one built is reviewed rather than invented under time pressure.

No swipe-to-confirm, no preselected destructive option, no generic “OK”, and the result is never auto-dismissed. The confirming control is styled as its own thing precisely so it cannot be mistaken for an ordinary primary button. Each step’s state is carried by a shape as well as a colour.

Data quality

§17.11 asks every energy view to state its completeness and freshness. A number computed from two of five meters is a different claim from the same number computed from all five, and the reader cannot tell them apart unless the view says so.

Data states

§20 requires every major screen to implement these. Each one explains what happened, says what is affected, shows the last successful update where it is relevant, and offers one recovery action. None of them says “something went wrong”, and none shows a normal state when the data to judge normality is missing.
